Drag on the left side to move, drag on the right side to look. Hold the big Fire button to aim and shoot at once. Jump, Dash, Duck, Reload and Nade sit around it, and you can press several at once; tap a weapon at the top to switch.

Online matches are played in 3-minute rounds once 2 players are in; you can jump in at any time, even mid-round. Ranked: +25 for each elimination (+5 for a headshot), +150 for winning a round, −15 for each death. From Champion up, eliminations give 15 and deaths cost 25.

Friends who type the same party code land in the same private match. Leave it blank to join the open arena.
