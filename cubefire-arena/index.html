How ranked works

Ranked puts you with players near your rank in lobbies of up to 6 (from Pro down to Worst Possible), up to 60 players at once. You only switch lobbies between rounds. Rounds last 3 minutes once 2 players are in, and every round is on a new map. Ranked points: +30 for each elimination (+5 for a headshot); a death costs 2 RP at Bronze, rising with rank to 28 at Legend; and for winning a round +100 (under 4 players) or +300 (4 or more); second place gets a third of that (+33 or +100). If everyone else leaves, the last player left wins the round.

Friends who type the same party code land in the same private match Parties are open to everyone, with no player limit, and the only way in is typing the code (you can't call friends to one); they give half the rank points (wins and losses) and no rare spins. Leave it blank to join ranked.
