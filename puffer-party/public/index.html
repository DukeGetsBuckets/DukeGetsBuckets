Puffer Party

A fast multiplayer word game

Join a room

How to play
  1. Everyone gets the same category, such as Name an animal.
  2. When the puffer points at you, you have 10 seconds to enter an answer nobody has used this round. A valid answer passes the puffer on.
  3. Run out of time and you lose a life.
  4. The puffer keeps inflating, with no visible timer. When it pops, whoever is holding it gets the rest of their time for one final answer. Miss it and you lose a life.
  5. A new round starts with a new category. The last player with lives remaining wins.

Answers are forgiving: minor typos, plurals and close synonyms all count.
