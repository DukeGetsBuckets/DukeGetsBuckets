Puffer Party

Name it. Pass it. Don't let it pop on you.

or join friends

How to play
  1. Everyone gets the same category, like “Name an animal.”
  2. When the puffer points at you, you have 10 seconds to type an answer nobody has said yet. A good answer passes the puffer on.
  3. Run out of time and you lose a life ❤️.
  4. The puffer keeps swelling. There's no timer, so watch it grow. When it pops, whoever's holding it gets the rest of their time for one last answer. Miss it and you lose a life.
  5. Then a new round starts with a new category. Last one with lives left wins.

Spelling doesn't have to be perfect, and close answers count: “puppy” works for dog.
