Answer Scan

One tap finds every question on the page you are looking at and highlights the answer. Runs from a Safari bookmark, so it works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Scan this page

On a Mac, drag the green button onto the Favorites bar and you are done. On iPhone or iPad use the steps below.

Install on iPhone / iPad

  1. Tap Copy bookmark code above.
  2. Tap Safari's Share button, choose Add Bookmark, name it Answer Scan, tap Save.
  3. Open Bookmarks (the book icon), tap Edit, tap the Answer Scan bookmark.
  4. Clear the address field, Paste the code, tap Done.
  5. On any quiz page: open Bookmarks and tap Answer Scan. Answers light up green.
