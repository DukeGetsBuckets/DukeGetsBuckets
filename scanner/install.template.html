Safari bookmarklet for iPad

Question Scanner

One tap on any page finds the questions on it, pulls out answers the page already contains, and can ask Claude for the rest. Build your copy below, then add it to Safari as a bookmark.

1. Build your bookmarklet

Without a key the scanner still finds questions, shows answers hidden in the page, and gives you a Search button for each question. With a key, the Ask AI button answers everything. The key is baked into the bookmark on your iPad and is only ever sent to api.anthropic.com. Get one at platform.claude.com. Never share a bookmark that contains your key.

On a Mac or PC you can skip the copying: drag this to your bookmarks bar instead. Scan questions

2. Add it to Safari on your iPad

  1. Tap Copy code above.
  2. Bookmark this page: tap the Share button, then Add Bookmark, name it Scan, and tap Save.
  3. Open your bookmarks (the book icon), tap Edit, then tap the Scan bookmark.
  4. Clear the Address field, paste the copied code into it, and tap Done.

Safari keeps the whole code in the bookmark, even though the address field only shows the start of it.

3. Use it

  1. Open the page with the questions.
  2. Open your bookmarks and tap Scan, or type scan in the address bar and tap the bookmark when it appears.
  3. A panel appears in the corner with every question it found. Green options were marked correct somewhere in the page. Tap Ask AI for Claude's answers, Search to look one up, or Show to jump to it.
  4. Tap the bookmark again after the page changes to rescan. Tap ✕ to close.

Good to know

How it finds answers in the page
  • Options tagged with attributes such as data-correct, data-answer, or classes like correct.
  • Hidden inputs and hidden text such as "Answer: B" next to the question.
  • Quiz data embedded in <script> JSON or in global JavaScript variables.

Many quiz sites only check answers on their server, so nothing is in the page. That is what Ask AI and Search are for.

Ask AI says it cannot reach the API

Some sites block outside requests. Use Copy for Claude in the panel and paste the questions into the Claude app instead. A 401 CORS message means your Claude organization does not allow browser requests at all.

Privacy and cost

Nothing leaves the page unless you tap Ask AI. That sends the questions, and by default the page text, to api.anthropic.com using your key, which you pay for per request. Turn off page context in the panel's settings (⚙︎) to send less.
